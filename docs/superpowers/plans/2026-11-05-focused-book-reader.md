# Focused iPad book reader

Use the existing authenticated book reader and ephemeral translation/read-aloud flow. Hide student navigation/footer only for book detail routes. A viewport-sized reading room keeps exit, book title, compact page navigation and Settings in one top bar. Reading speed and local English voices move into an accessible settings dialog. Fit the original page to available space, retaining native pinch zoom. Put speakers beside text rather than masking it.

Horizontal single-finger swipes on the book turn pages with a short directional animation; ignore vertical scrolls, multi-touch, zoomed viewports, interactive controls, cancelled/slow gestures and system navigation edges. Keep first/last boundaries and accessible top buttons. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Clear temporary translation/feedback and cancel recordings when changing pages.

The passage dock is compact and expandable: beside the book in landscape, below it in portrait. Direct sentence speakers or previous/next passage buttons select text; word meanings, Listen, Meaning and Read aloud stay within reach. Automatically expand for translation/recording feedback, with internal scrolling for long text. No new persistence or paid calls.

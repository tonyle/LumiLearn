# Magic Sort Academy
Approved conversation: twenty gently increasing levels, basic educational practice, enjoyable for children, no speed pressure. Five four-level chapters grow from two colours/two layers to four colours/four layers, retaining two spare bottles throughout. Tap source/destination; Undo, restart and hints always available. Two or three short math/word seals per level. Wrong responses explain and retry; missed content revisits after another question. Parent selects number/word/mixed focus and basic math group, with grade-based defaults. Assigned vocabulary/images/audio are reused; built-in simple words are a fallback. No live AI calls.
Server validates moves, answers, unlocks and timers. Persist unfinished boards and completed gardens per student. Shared points-to-time wallet, daily caps and parent game pause remain authoritative. Twenty flowers/stars are cosmetic; game practice never grants spendable learning points or marks worksheets complete. Leaving or time expiry saves confirmed work; remaining time refunded by existing game finish flow. Personal level map/companion; sound toggle and reduced motion support.
